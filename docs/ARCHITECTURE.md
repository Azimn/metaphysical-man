# MADMAN Architecture 0.1

## Metaphysical Artificial Dynamics for Metabolic Autopoiesis and Noesis

MADMAN replaces the earlier PEMA working name. PEMA began as an internal cognitive economy in which already recognizable cognitive actors competed for resources. MADMAN makes a more radical commitment: recognizable cognitive categories are not primitive. The substrate comes first, and the research question is whether mind-like regularities can become stable descriptions of what the substrate does.

The architecture is not a model of the human brain. It is not an ontological claim about consciousness. It is an engineered digital universe inspired by metaphysics, artificial life, homeostasis, resource-rational cognition, dynamical systems, and the biological fact that persistent organization is expensive.

## The Crucible

The Crucible is the total simulated substrate. It is a directed graph of local sites connected by conductive edges. The graph is visible to the external researcher but is not represented inside the simulated world as a globally inspectable object. Sites react only to events that arrive locally. Edges constrain what can reach them.

A software scheduler advances the Crucible, but the scheduler is treated as physics. It may enforce resource conservation, apply local transformation laws, advance decay, and record instrumentation. It may not choose what matters, select cognitive winners, create memories, assign emotions, decide goals, or declare a self.

## Sparks

A Spark is a transient occasion. It carries a normalized signature, an intensity, a local destination, causal parent identifiers for the external trace, an origin label for instrumentation, and a bounded propagation generation. A Spark is not a thought. It is merely a local event that may or may not acquire enough metabolic support to influence anything else.

A Spark arriving at a site is not guaranteed assimilation. Assimilation has a local cost that rises with Ash and with the work implied by the incoming intensity. The site has a local receptivity. Resonance between the incoming signature and the site's receptivity changes how strongly the Spark alters local activation. If Aether cannot pay the cost, the Spark dies before becoming a complete local event.

This implements the rule that cognition cannot be completed for free before competition occurs.

## The four alchemical resources

Aether is immediately spendable flux. It pays for assimilation and propagation. Resource sources inject Aether at specific locations, and Aether diffuses through the topology according to local gradients and effective conductance.

Ichor is bound reserve. Strong local activation can capture a small portion of available Aether into Ichor. Active sites can later release Ichor back into Aether. Ichor decays slowly. This lets past activation change the local probability of future reconstruction without creating a memory record.

Azoth is structural potential. It pays for durable changes in connectivity. When connected sites are sufficiently coactive, some Azoth is consumed and the connecting edge receives a Scar. During sufficiently quiet, low-residue periods, a small quantity of Aether can convert back into Azoth. This creates a simple local chemistry in which immediate processing and long-term plasticity compete for the same underlying budget.

Ash is inhibitory residue. Assimilation and propagation convert part of their metabolic cost into Ash. Ash raises the cost of continued local processing and decreases effective conductivity. Ash dissipates over time. There is no fatigue variable. Fatigue-like behavior can only arise from depletion and residue.

## Scars

A Scar is persistent deformation on an edge. Repeated coactivation can deepen a Scar, increasing effective conductance after the original activation has vanished. Scars decay much more slowly than activation.

The Crucible contains no primitive memory store. If a later partial perturbation travels more easily because earlier activity changed Scars, the resulting reconstruction is called an Echo by the observer. The system itself does not retrieve an Echo object.

This is intended to make remembering a reconstruction problem rather than a database lookup.

## Hauntings

A Haunting is not a class in the code. It is an observer-level description for a recurrent configuration that repeatedly captures enough resource to rebuild itself. A Haunting can be supported by Ichor, Scars, recurrent connectivity, Morrow, and local resource sources. It can also destroy the conditions of its own continuation by accumulating Ash or exhausting local resource.

A recurrent pattern that never relinquishes resource is not automatically considered a bug. Permanent capture is a valid experimental result. The architecture does not contain a fairness governor that rotates attention merely because one configuration has been active for too long.

## Morrow

Morrow is the most deliberately speculative field in MADMAN. It is a local scalar representing prospective resource pressure. Resource sources create Morrow. Nearby sites inherit part of neighboring Morrow through existing conductive edges. The field relaxes locally over time.

Propagation is modestly biased toward sites with stronger Morrow. The resulting system can therefore behave as though future resource-rich continuations exert present attraction even though no goal object, route planner, or backward causation exists.

The implementation is intentionally conservative mathematically. Morrow is just a locally propagated potential field. Its metaphysical interpretation is optional. Its behavioral consequences are testable.

## Constellations

A Constellation is an observer-detected cluster of simultaneously active sites with sufficiently strong effective coupling. Constellations are not objects used by the substrate. GhostHunter can estimate their membership, internal closure, activation, and resource share after the fact.

This allows the experiment to ask whether temporary higher-order causal units appear without first implementing a workspace or binding controller.

## The Medium

The Medium is a deterministic first-person reporting layer intended to model epistemic disadvantage. Only sites explicitly designated as report surfaces are visible to it. The Medium receives activation, a local signature, and local familiarity from those surfaces. It cannot inspect hidden resources, Scars, topology, source locations, causal parentage, or the trace.

Its statements are therefore interpretations of consequences. A hidden Scar can cause something to feel familiar without the Medium being able to name the structural reason. This keeps introspective uncertainty architectural rather than theatrical.

A future LLM can replace the deterministic verbal renderer, but only through the same restricted interface.

## The GhostHunter

GhostHunter is external instrumentation. It can calculate resource-flow entropy, identify metabolic dominance, and detect temporary Constellations. The Crucible cannot call GhostHunter. The Medium cannot call GhostHunter. This enforces the distinction between third-person explanation and organism-relative access.

## Branching and fusion

A Crucible can be cloned exactly. A clone begins with the same local state and topology but can receive a different random seed and different future perturbations. This supports identity and developmental-divergence experiments.

Compatible Crucibles can also be fused externally. Fusion blends current activation, local resources, signatures, Morrow, conductance, and Scars. The resulting substrate receives no metaphysical declaration about whether it is one predecessor, both predecessors, or a new individual. That question is left to behavioral analysis.

## Communication

VeilChannel implements communication as perturbation rather than state transfer. A sender can produce a Glyph from a local active pattern. The channel introduces a stable recipient-side distortion before injecting the Glyph into another Crucible. The receiver therefore does not receive the sender's internal state. It receives a perturbation that must interact with its own topology and history.

Meaning is therefore not copied. Similarity of interpretation is an empirical outcome.

## Observer language and implementation language

Terms such as memory-like, attention-like, concern-like, self-like, personality-like, or emotion-like are descriptions available to researchers after observing dynamics. They are not licensed as primitive mechanisms merely because the resulting behavior resembles those concepts.

The anti-normalization rule remains simple: do not repair an interesting failure until we know whether the failure is the phenomenon.
